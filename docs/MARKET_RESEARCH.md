# JobMail market notes

**Checked:** 2026-11-17. Prices are public US list prices captured today and may change. This is desk research, not customer validation. Review comments are individual reports; they are not universal product findings.

## Target user and problem

Job seekers who receive application, interview, and offer updates by email and want those milestones organized in a private tracker. Current product reads Gmail locally and can append qualifying message details to a user-owned Google Sheet. It is not an auto-apply service and it does not promise interviews.

## Positioning hypothesis

**JobMail turns opted-in Gmail job updates into a private, spreadsheet-owned timeline, with a read-only preview before any write and no inbox-body storage by default.** This differentiates on control and traceability, not AI resume claims or application volume. Confirm that users value the local-first/Gmail-to-Sheets workflow before setting a paid offer.

## Validation questions

1. Which job updates do users most often miss or re-enter by hand?
2. Would they connect Gmail to a local tool with read-only preview first? What permission would make them stop?
3. Is a Google Sheet a benefit or a setup burden?
4. Would they pay for guided OAuth/filter setup, or expect the product to remain free?
5. Which messages should never be copied into the sheet?
